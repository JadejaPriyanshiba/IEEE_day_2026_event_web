00 — The concept

Out of
spec.

IEEE is famous for standards — dry documents that make the world interoperate. The site borrows that voice: section numbers, figure captions, clauses, a “Draft 01” stamp. Then it lets five very un-standard things happen inside it. The joke is structural: the more official it looks, the funnier it gets that it's a Garba invite.

0–10 s“Wait. What is this?”A whistle, a notice, and a headline claiming something at VGEC is out of spec. No event name shouting at you.
10–40 s“Oh… this is IEEE Day?”The navy reveal: IEEE makes the rules. Today it breaks five. The date lands as a 470px “06”.
Explore“This looks ridiculously fun.”Five chapters, five grounds, five signals. Each one breaks the page's rhythm in its own way.
Decide“I want to go.”Tickets, not a form wall. what → when → who → fee → one verb that matches the event.
§ 1Official voice, unofficial content.Clause numbers, figure captions and metadata do the framing. Headlines do the mischief. Never explain the joke.
§ 2Type is the illustration.No stock photos, so huge statements, outlined numerals and line drawings carry the page. Tiny metadata sits beside giant words.
§ 3Every motion has an alibi.Things move when they mean something: a whistle wobbles, a clue declassifies, the Garba circle turns. Everything else stays still.

Headlines ignore the gutters on purpose; metadata snaps hard to them. Events live as cards on one 6-column grid (one card per swipe on mobile), each with its own ground colour and one anchor object. Vertical rhythm alternates: dense ledger → empty statement → dense ledger.

Square corners, no shadows, no pills. One verb per event, never “Register Now”. Chapter CTAs take the chapter's contrast colour (gold on navy, navy on gold, ivory on terracotta); ticket CTAs are always terracotta so the Register section reads as one decision.

05 — Event vocabulary
01 HuntNavy night. Whistle, dotted trail, redaction bars, outlined numeral. Signal: jagged.
02 WorkshopIvory + grid paper. Notebook page, hand diagram, arrows. Signal: smooth sine.
03 BrandGold ground. Construction grid, circles, ratios, one filled mark. Signal: square wave.
04 CharadesBrown ink. Speech bubble, banned words struck through. Signal: noise.
05 GarbaTerracotta ground. Concentric circles, dancers as dots, crossed dandiya. Signal: loops.

Drawn as 1.6–3px navy line work with round caps — technical-drawing weight, never clip-art fills. One filled accent per drawing (the whistle pea, the logo lens). Texture comes from grid paper, dashed rules and perforations, not noise overlays.
