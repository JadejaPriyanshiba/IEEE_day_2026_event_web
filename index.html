04Rules & Guidelines

General Guidelines. For all participants.

Essential campus protocols, eligibility criteria, and event codes for IEEE Day 2026.

Campus protocol

Guidelines for All Participants

  • Open to All Branches: Open to all enrolled VGEC students across all departments and academic years. No prior IEEE membership is required.
  • Punctuality & Reporting: Report at the respective event venue 15 minutes prior to scheduled start time. Reporting for Cluestle Hunt is 8:30 AM sharp at A Block.
  • Mandatory Student ID: Carrying your physical VGEC Student ID Card is mandatory for entry, participation check-in, and receiving certificates.
  • Fair Play & Campus Decorum: Maintain respectful campus conduct and academic integrity. Decisions of judges and event coordinators are final.
  • Participation Formats: Solo / Individual participation for LogoLabz and Tech Charades; team-based for Cluestle Hunt; open celebration for Garba.
  • Official Rulebooks: Download the Cluestle Hunt Official Rulebook (PDF) ↗ and the LogoLabz Official Rulebook (PDF) ↗.

05FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still stuck? Call one of the humans in the footer.

I'm from a different branch or year. Am I still eligible?

Yes. All VGEC students from any branch and any year are welcome. IEEE Day is not a department event; it belongs to the whole campus.

Do I need to be an IEEE member?

No. IEEE Day is open to all VGEC students. Joining IEEE is encouraged but never a barrier to participating.

Can I register for more than one event?

Yes, of course. Check the schedule before registering for multiple events to make sure timeslots don't overlap.

Can I come alone, or do I need a team?

Depends on the event. Cluestle Hunt is team-based. LogoLabz, the NLP Workshop, and Tech Charades are individual participation. Garba is open to everyone: solo, duo, or the whole friend group.

Do I need to bring a laptop?

Not strictly required for all events. The NLP Workshop with Dr. Uttam Chauhan explores machine learning concepts, so bringing a laptop is helpful if you wish to follow along hands-on. For LogoLabz, refer to the official rulebook for design submission guidelines.

Will there be certificates or prizes?

Yes. Participation certificates for all, and prizes/hard copy certificates for winners of competitive events. Details to be announced closer to the date.
